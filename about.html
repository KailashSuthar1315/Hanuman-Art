OUR LEGACY

THE STORY OF HANUMAN ART

Handcrafting Masterpieces for Over 35 Years

Based out of the historic furniture hub of Jodhpur, Rajasthan, Hanuman Art has been dedicated to delivering high-quality, durable, and intricately carved wooden furniture. We specialize in working with seasoned premium timbers like Teak and Rosewood (Sheesham) to ensure generational longevity.

"Quality, trust, and perfection are embedded into every single piece of wood that leaves our workshop floor."

Whether it is traditional Indian carvings for architectural palace entries and royal pooja mandirs or elegant sleek minimalist furniture lines for modern luxury homes, our master artisans translate conceptual drawings into real-world landmarks.
